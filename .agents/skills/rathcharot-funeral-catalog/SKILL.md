# Rathcharot Funeral Photography & Live Streaming Catalog Skill

This skill defines the exact visual identity, content structure, image asset mapping, and copywriting rules for the **ราชรถ (Rathcharot) & ช่างภาพงานศพ.com** Presentation Catalog.

## 1. Visual Theme & Brand Identity (Reference Style)

Based on the official Rathcharot catalog reference:

### Color Palette
- **Background Primary**: Deep Obsidian / Charcoal Black (`#0D0D0D` to `#141414`) with a subtle radial vignette (`radial-gradient(circle at center, #1C1A17 0%, #0A0A0A 100%)`).
- **Accent Gold (Primary Heading & Borders)**: Thai Royal Gold (`#D4AF37`, `#C5A059`, with metallic gradient `linear-gradient(135deg, #F3E5AB 0%, #D4AF37 50%, #996515 100%)`).
- **Text Primary (Body & Subtitles)**: Pure Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) and Warm Off-White (`#F5F2EB`).
- **Watermark / Background Motif**: Traditional Thai Kranok / Ratcharot emblem rendered at `4%–7%` opacity (`opacity: 0.06`) centered or positioned in the lower-left/center of each slide/page.

### Frame & Ornamental Elements
- **Outer Border**: Thin dual or single gold hairline frame (`1px solid #C5A059`) inset `20px–32px` from the viewport/page edge.
- **Corner Ornaments**: Concave scooped corners (`border-radius` cutout effect or SVG corner flourishes) featuring a golden 8-petal Thai lotus/star rosette (`ประจำยาม`) at all four corners, plus a top-center and bottom-center lotus emblem.
- **Dividers**: Horizontal golden ornamental divider lines tapering at both ends with a central infinity/diamond lotus knot (`─── ◇ ───` or SVG equivalent).

### Typography
- **Font Family**: Google Fonts `'Prompt'` or `'Kanit'` (for clean, modern geometric Thai readability matching the reference) paired with `'Sarabun'` or `'Cormorant Garamond'` for numerals.
- **Hierarchy**:
  - **Section Number / Index**: Large gold numeral (`3.5rem–4.5rem`, `#D4AF37`, `font-weight: 600`).
  - **Section Title**: Gold (`1.8rem–2.5rem`, `#D4AF37`, `font-weight: 600`, letter-spacing `0.02em`).
  - **Key Selling Hook / Subtitle**: Bold White (`1.15rem–1.35rem`, `#FFFFFF`, `font-weight: 500`).
  - **Bullet Points**: Clean `- ` list in `#F0EDE6` (`1rem–1.1rem`, line-height `1.7`).

## 2. Copywriting Philosophy ("Scannable & High-Impact")

- **NO Walls of Text**: Customers should **never** have to read long paragraphs.
- **3-Second Scroll Rule**: As a client scrolls or flips through, they must immediately see:
  1. **ตอนนี้เป็นพิธีอะไร** (What ceremony/service this is)
  2. **ทำไมต้องใช้ช่างภาพมืออาชีพแบบเรา** (Why they need a specialized ceremonial team like us — 3 to 4 short, punchy bullet points max per section).
- **Let the Photos Speak**: Use large, high-resolution grids, hero showcases, or smooth carousels/lightboxes organized strictly by the workspace folders.
